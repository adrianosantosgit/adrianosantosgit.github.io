01 / the beginning

Curiosity became a craft.

My connection with software engineering started around 2004, when MMORPGs made me curious about what happened behind the screen. I began creating local servers so friends and I could play together. That early mix of experimentation, problem-solving, and community still shapes how I learn and build.

Since 2019, I’ve been studying IT-related subjects at Brazilian universities, steadily expanding that curiosity into a broader practice: understanding systems, asking better questions, and making useful things work well.
